About

Basma Gado holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Cinema & Media Arts from York University. Her work spans between storytelling, 3D animation, virtual production, video/audio editing, and media production. Driven by a passion for visual narratives, Basma has hands-on experience with tools across the full production pipeline, from motion capture to real-time rendering. She is eager to apply her academic knowledge in real-world settings and continue expanding her skills through engaging, collaborative projects.
